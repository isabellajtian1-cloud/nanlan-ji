# V3.0 人物素材与接入说明

采用内置 image_gen 生成一张透明底 4×4 人物图集，再按单元格切成 WebP。未使用 CLI 或外部人物素材。参考本项目 character.png 与 village.webp 的画风；人物为本项目原创设定的视觉表达。

文件路径：dist/assets/npc-{角色键}-v30.webp；GitHub 发行包中的路径为 assets/npc-{角色键}-v30.webp。

角色键按图集行序：grandfather、father、mother、chen / doctor、cen、yu、wan / qiu、lin、tea、vendor / playerMale、playerFemale、guard、customer。

这些是 2D 静态人物精灵，不是 3D 网格或绑定模型。主角仍由游戏代码驱动移动和轻微步态起伏；NPC 随剧情显示或隐藏。人物按脚点 y 排序，保持与主角相同的 84 设计像素高度，软接触阴影与昼夜明暗由画布生成。第一卷父亲出门时，正厅人物和地图入口一同隐藏。第二卷许青禾在百草堂；每日来客用尽时，铺内客人消失。

## Final prompt / built-in image_gen

Create ONE production game sprite atlas, a precise 4 column by 4 row grid of 16 full-body people on a genuinely transparent background, large square canvas. References are STYLE ONLY: match the warm detailed hand-painted Ming-era Lingnan village game, the fine brushwork, natural adult anatomy, elegant cloth folds and subdued jade, ivory, indigo and earth palette. NOT cartoon, not chibi, not vector, not photorealistic modern people. All people seen from gently elevated 3/4 camera, head to shoes fully visible, standing facing southeast towards viewer. All cells exactly equal square dimensions, each person centered with entire body confined to middle 65% width and 82% height of their cell; same foot baseline 90% down each cell, generous transparent gaps; no objects crossing cells, no labels, no borders, no scenery or floor patches, no baked shadows. Clean alpha edges without coloured fringes.
